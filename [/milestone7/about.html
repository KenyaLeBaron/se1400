About the Author

I grew up being the only girl in my family amongst a sea of brothers. I have four older brothers and one younger brother from both of my parents. I also have two half brothers, one from my dads side and one from my moms side, and one step brother. Growing up with so many brothers might seem weird to some people, but it’s all I’ve ever known. It really is an interesting family dynamic but I wouldn’t change it for the world.

In Conclusion

My family history is a little crazy, and I’m pretty ok with that. It makes for some pretty interesting stories. Sharing about my family has always been something I could initiate at any time at any point in any conversation. I find it easy to talk about such an intricate story that can captivate its listeners, and that’s why I created this Website. To share something I find very intriguing.

I hope that I can pass these stories down so that my children can learn about their ancestry, and be able to see how far we’ve come. I want them to learn from their older generations' mistakes, and make a better life for themselves.
